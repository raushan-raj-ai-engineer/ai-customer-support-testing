# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping normally takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express shipping normally takes 1 to 2 business days.

Customers receive a tracking number after the order has been shipped.

Orders that are still in PROCESSING status may not yet have a tracking number.

Delivery times may vary because of weather, courier delays, public holidays, or remote delivery locations.

Customers should contact support when a shipment is significantly delayed beyond the estimated delivery date.
