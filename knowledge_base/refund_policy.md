# Refund and Return Policy

Customers may return eligible products within 30 days of the original purchase date.

The product should be returned in reasonable condition with the original accessories whenever applicable.

After the returned product is inspected and approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing normally takes 5 to 7 business days after approval.

Products damaged because of customer misuse may not qualify for a full refund.

For defective products, customers should contact customer support for replacement or refund assistance.
